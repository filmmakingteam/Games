Rules

Tap the draw pile or top discard to take a card, optionally lay a set/run or add cards to an existing meld, then discard.

Set: 3+ same rank, distinct suits.
Run: 3+ consecutive ranks, same suit. Ace is low (A-2-3).
Add to meld: Cards must legally extend a same-suit consecutive run, or match a set's rank with unused suits.
Win: First player to empty their hand wins.
When the draw pile runs out, the discard pile is shuffled back into a new draw pile, keeping its top card available for play.
